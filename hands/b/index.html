What ages your hands?

  • Dark spots

    Years of sun (or tanning lamps) speed up melanin production, and brown spots appear on the hands.

  • Dry, cracked skin

    Weather, frequent handwashing and chemicals all dry out the skin on your hands.

  • Wrinkles

    Hands are rarely moisturized or protected with sunscreen, and their thin skin ages fast.
